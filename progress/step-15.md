### What Step 15 inherits from Step 13, in one paragraph

Step 13 wired the assertion into the run-control state machine at one place.
`RunController.move(to:)` is the sole assignment to `state`, and it asks
`RunControlPolicy.preventsIdleSleep(in:)` about the destination: the assertion is held in
`.running` and nowhere else, so it is released on the transition out of running, whatever the
ending — pause, stop, completion, failure or device loss. Both halves log beside the
`run control:` line of the transition that caused them —
`sleep prevention: holding an idle-system-sleep assertion for the run …` and
`sleep prevention: released the idle-system-sleep assertion` — and both were seen on hardware in
Step 13's walk. **A Step 15 change that edits `move(to:)`, `preventsIdleSleep(in:)` or
`IdleSleepPreventer`, or assigns `state` anywhere else, lapses Step 13's gate by its own
invalidation list**, and the re-walk is `progress/step-13-human-checklist.md`. Two of the
assertion's failures have no automated cover — deleting the release call (m7) and holding the
display-sleep type instead (m8) each pass the whole suite — and that checklist is what checks

so a hold is read while it is held, from `pmset -g assertions` matched on the pid or from
`scripts/sleep-assertion-watch.sh` — which leaves the app's own `sleep prevention:` lines as the
only after-the-fact record of every hold.
